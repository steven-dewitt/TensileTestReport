3 Specimen dimensions

Measure the gauge-section diameter twice at the minimum section, 90° apart. The cross-sectional area comes from the mean diameter and is used to check the reported stress at break.

5 Summary of results

Statistics, all included specimens

Values are stored in metric and converted for display, so switching units never changes the data. Statistics include only specimens marked Included. SD is the sample standard deviation (n − 1). CV = SD / mean. For the Weibull fit of tensile strength, rank i of n uses F = (i − 0.5)/n and least-squares regression of ln ln[1/(1−F)] on ln σ.
