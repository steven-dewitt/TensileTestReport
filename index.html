3 Test setup (load train)

The specimen is loaded in tension through a flexible load train, from top to bottom:

  1. Upper crosshead: moves upward to apply the load (arrow).
  2. Strained chains, 4 links or more: let the load train self-align so the specimen is pulled in line, with minimal bending.
  3. Optional parasitic stress monitor: detects bending (parasitic) stresses in the load train.
  4. Upper grip: holds the upper end of the specimen.
  5. Specimen: round tensile specimen (see section 2).
  6. Lower grip: holds the lower end, connected by a precision chain attachment.
  7. Strained chains, 4 links or more, joined to the lower crosshead by a second precision chain attachment.

4 Specimen dimensions

Measure the gauge-section diameter twice at the minimum section, 90° apart. The cross-sectional area comes from the mean diameter and is used to check the reported stress at break.

Statistics, all included specimens
