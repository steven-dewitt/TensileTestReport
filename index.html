2 Specimen location in billet

Test specimens are machined from an extruded graphite billet, Ø7.750 in × 1.000 in thick. Each specimen is 4.750 in long, with 0.500 in diameter ends and a 0.250 in diameter centre section. It lies on a diameter through the centre of the billet at mid-thickness, perpendicular to the extrusion axis, with 1.500 in of billet left at each end. Views are drawn to scale.

3 Specimen dimensions

Measure the gauge-section diameter twice at the minimum section, 90° apart. The cross-sectional area comes from the mean diameter and is used to check the reported stress at break.

Statistics, all included specimens
